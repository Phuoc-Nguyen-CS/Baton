Keys

  up k         Move up (on the list) or scroll
  down j       Move down (on the list) or scroll
  pgup         Scroll a page up
  pgdn         Scroll a page down
  enter        Open the selected task
  esc          Go back
  y            Allow the task's pending permission request
  n            Deny it, with an optional reason for the worker
  a            Accept the candidate (asks to confirm); it stays on baton/<task>
  c            Ask the worker for changes
  d            Decide later
  v            View the candidate's full diff
  t            Take over the worker's terminal (claude attach); detach to return
  ?            Show this help
  q ctrl-c     Quit; workers keep running

Enter never grants a permission or accepts work: those take their own key.
